# 6. Environments (3 levels)

See REF-ENV-1/2/3 in Appendix B. Each level is a horizontally scrolling side-view course, 5 to 6 screens wide (about
2400 to 2900 px), built from a modular kit of tiles and props drawn in the reference style. REF-ENV-2 (subway) and
REF-ENV-3 (warehouse) are already side-on and may be downscaled to 480x270 and used as the first-screen mid-layer,
then extended with kit pieces and mirror-tiled seams. REF-ENV-1 (alley) is drawn in one-point perspective: use it as
title/loading art and as a style target, and rebuild the playable alley side-on from the same elements and palette.
Level 1: LAST CALL ALLEY (night, tutorial-friendly) - REF-ENV-1
Setting
Narrow night alley between brick tenements. Sodium streetlamps cast dithered amber cones on asphalt.
Magenta and cyan neon spill on the ground. Distant skyline with lit windows.
Visual kit
Red-brown brick walls with graffiti tags; fire escape with railings; roll-up garage door with graffiti; green
dumpsters (one with open lid); metal trash cans; cardboard boxes; concrete entrance steps; neon signs
ARCADE (red box sign) and vertical LIQUOR (cyan); parked cars in the distance; red-and-white striped curb
barrier.
Skate objects
Wooden kicker/quarter ramp x2, flat metal grind rail x3, dumpster lid ledge, curb grind, stair set (gap),
fire-escape rail (high line reached from the kicker), flat manual pad.
Gaps (bonus)
Dumpster Gap (jump the open dumpster), Stair Set, Barrier Hop (striped curb).
Collectibles
S-K-A-T-E letters x5 (some require tricks to reach); secret Arcade Token hidden on the upper fire-escape
route near the ARCADE sign.
Hazards
Rolling trash cans (collision = slowdown or bail). Otherwise none. Teaches fundamentals.
Mood / audio
Lo-fi synthwave, distant city hum, neon buzz.
Level 2: PLATFORM ZERO (subway station) - REF-ENV-2
Setting
Underground platform. Sage-green tiled wall covered in colourful graffiti pieces (pink, green, blue, teal,
orange), rusty ceiling pipes, warm fluorescent fixtures with pooled light on cracked concrete, yellow safety
line, track bed below.
Visual kit
Dark-green and maroon train with orange-lit windows and a small flag/logo; metal bench; wooden kicker
ramp; flat box with spray cans; scattered cans; support pillars; ceiling cables.
Skate objects
Kicker ramps, bench grind, platform-edge ledge grind (the yellow line is safe, the track bed is a bail zone),
flat box (funbox), manual pad.
Dynamic element
A train arrives and leaves every ~25 s on the track bed (animated in the background plane, with wind gust
and audio cue). It never hurts the player but the track bed does (bail zone). A Track Gap lets the player clear
the tracks to a far platform island.
Gaps (bonus)
Track Gap, Bench Hop, Pillar Gap.
Collectibles
S-K-A-T-E letters x5; Spray Cans x5 hidden around the level: collecting all five reveals a new graffiti piece
on the wall (secret goal); a hidden Metro Token.
Mood / audio
Dark electro, 110 BPM, echoing percussion, tunnel reverb, train rumble.
Level 3: THE FOUNDRY (warehouse skatepark) - REF-ENV-3
Setting
Disused brick warehouse converted into a skatepark. Steel I-beam columns and trusses, hanging
green-white fluorescent tubes (flicker), cracked concrete floor, graffiti on exposed brick.
Visual kit
Big grey concrete quarter-pipe; orange-rust wooden kickers and funbox with blue-grey side panels; crates;
stacked tires; pallets; green and rust oil drums; green industrial machinery (control box and lathe-like
machine).
Skate objects
Large concrete quarter-pipes (vert-style air), funbox with grindable edges, machinery top (ledge grind), crate
stack (hip), manual lines, drum slalom.
Dynamic element
A conveyor belt section (speed boost or drag zone) and rolling oil drums released at intervals.
Gaps (bonus)
Big Air (across the main bowl), Funbox Transfer, Machine Hop.
Collectibles
S-K-A-T-E letters x5 (two require big air); hidden Golden Wrench on a high beam reachable via a vert ramp.
Mood / audio
Industrial breakbeat, 124 BPM, metallic reverb, machine hum.


Level layout rule: design each level as a sequence of lines (ramp -> air -> rail -> manual -> gap) with a safe low route and
a risky high route. Provide at least two ways to chain a 10-trick combo without touching the ground.
