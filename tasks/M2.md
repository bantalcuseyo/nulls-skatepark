# M2 — Terrain and ramps

Read: `docs/spec/07_gameplay.md` (§7.3, §7.6), `docs/spec/10_architecture.md` (level schema).

## Deliverable
Polyline ground with slopes, kicker and quarter-pipe props, momentum conservation, ramp launch,
landing evaluation (clean / sketchy / bail), bail and respawn at the last safe position.

## Files
- `src/sim/Terrain.ts`, `src/sim/Physics.ts` (continuous collision), `src/sim/Landing.ts`
- `src/entities/Prop.ts` (Ramp, Rail, Ledge), `src/systems/LevelLoader.ts`
- `data/levels/alley.json` (geometry only for now)
- `tests/terrain.test.ts`, `tests/landing.test.ts`

## Acceptance checks
- [ ] Ride up and launch off a quarter-pipe (tangent velocity preserved)
- [ ] No tunnelling at max speed (swept collision test at 300 px/s toward a thin rail)
- [ ] Landing angle thresholds: 25° clean, 50° bail
- [ ] Bail costs the combo, ~1.0 s, respawn rolling at last safe ground
- [ ] Skater can never leave the world
