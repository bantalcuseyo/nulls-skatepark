# M8 — Audio and juice

Read: `docs/spec/09_audio.md`, `docs/spec/07_gameplay.md` (§7.9), `game-feel` skill rules.

## Deliverable
Music loops (menu 80, alley 96, subway 110, warehouse 124 BPM), full SFX list, trauma-based
shake, hit-stop, particles, light flicker, CRT toggle, camera polish, adaptive quality for phone.

## Files
- `src/systems/AudioManager.ts`, `src/systems/Music.ts` (WebAudio step sequencer)
- `src/systems/Particles.ts`, `src/systems/AdaptiveQuality.ts`
- `src/scenes/Settings.ts` (volume buses, shake/flash reduction, CRT)
- `tests/audio.test.ts` (sequencer pattern purity)

## Acceptance checks
- [ ] Mix balanced; master/music/SFX buses separate; no clipping
- [ ] Shake decays in real time (not scaled), reduce-shake/reduce-flash toggles work
- [ ] Hit-stop under 0.15 s, overlapping stops safe
- [ ] Phone holds near 60 FPS or steps down quality gracefully (frame-time measured over 2 s)
- [ ] Audio unlocks on first input; resumes after visibilitychange on iOS
